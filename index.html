Santa Clara County Parks Trails and Restrooms

This map displays trails and restrooms located in Santa Clara County Parks to help users explore recreational routes and nearby facilities.

The trail and restroom data are loaded from live ArcGIS feature services provided by Santa Clara County Parks: Trails and Points of Interest. The original ArcGIS sample was customized to display this data by changing the map’s center, basemap, symbols, and popup information. Trail-difficulty filters with buttons, a dynamic trail count, and a search component were also added to make the map easier to explore.

Click a trail or restroom on the map for more information.
